Active check — exposed sensitive files

QA-09 is the only check that sends requests. It performs up to 14 GET requests for well-known paths (/.env, /.git/HEAD, …) against the origin of the tab you're on. No crawling, no wordlists, no other host — ever.

Only enable this for sites you own or are explicitly authorised to test. Unsolicited probing of third-party servers may be unlawful where you live and will show up in their logs as your IP address.

One-time purchase. Unlocks HTML/PDF report export, saved custom check sets, and scan history.

Licence keys verify offline against a public key built into the extension. No account, no server, nothing phones home.

Privacy

QuickAudit sends exactly one kind of data off your machine: detected library name@version strings, to api.osv.dev, to look up known CVEs. No URLs, no page content, no cookie values, no analytics, no accounts. Scan results are stored in local extension storage only.
